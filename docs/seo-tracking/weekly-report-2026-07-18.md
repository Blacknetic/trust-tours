**Plain-English read:** Google is now showing us **more than twice as often** as when we started, and clicks are up too. ## 4. The best sign this week: Google is pointing us at our own plan

Two things happened that tell us we're aimed correctly:

1. **Google's dashboard flagged that our "machame route" page got 385% more impressions** — and that page is scheduled for a full upgrade next week. The demand is real and rising.
2. **A cluster of 34 different "how much does it cost to climb Kilimanjaro" searches** is now landing on our cost page (e.g. *"kilimanjaro costs," "kilimanjaro climb price," "cost to climb mt kilimanjaro"*). This is exactly the page we plan to turn into a detailed cost breakdown next week.

Our best-performing search term for *actually getting clicks* is **"tour operators in arusha"** — real buyers looking for a local operator, which is precisely the audience the site is built for.

**Also notable:** one visitor this week arrived via an **AI assistant** (ChatGPT/AI search) — early proof that the AI-readiness work is starting to pay off.
