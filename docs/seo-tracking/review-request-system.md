# Review-request system (for Ombeni)

**Why this is the single highest-leverage habit.** Reviews are the one thing competitors beat us on and the one thing money can't buy — only finished trips can. Every trip that ends without a review ask is a review lost forever. During peak season that's several a week. This turns "keep asking" into a 30-second reflex.

It also feeds the website directly: a great new review gets its best line added to the tour page, which is what earns the ★ star ratings in Google search results (see "Close the loop" below).

## The rule

**2–3 days after each trip ends** (they're home, buzzing, photos sorted), send one message with a one-tap link. That's it. Same for every trip, every time.

## Make it a habit (so it doesn't fade in peak season)

- Add "send review request" as the **last line of the trip checklist** — the trip isn't "done" until it's sent.
- Set a **weekly reminder** to send to anyone who finished that week.
- Ask the guide to mention it warmly on the last day ("we'd love a review") so the message isn't a surprise.
- Never offer anything in exchange for a review — against the platforms' rules and unnecessary. A genuine ask from Ombeni converts well on its own.

## Close the loop → website stars (this is Day 32)

When a strong new review comes in on Google or TripAdvisor:

1. Copy the best one or two sentences, the reviewer's first name + last initial, the platform and month.
2. Send them to dev, or add them to the matching trip in `data/packages.ts` under `reviewSnippets` (the format is already there on the Machame page).
3. That review then appears on the tour page **and** becomes eligible for a ★ star rating in Google search — the biggest click-through advantage we have over OTA listings.

We currently have review snippets on **1** tour page. Getting genuine quotes onto our top **3–5 flagship trips** (Machame, Lemosho, a Great Migration safari, a Northern Circuit safari) is all that's needed to light up stars across the money pages.
