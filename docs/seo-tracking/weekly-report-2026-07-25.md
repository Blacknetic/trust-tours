## 4. Where we need to increase power (the priorities)

1. **Break onto page 1 for the terms we already appear for.** We're shown 217 times for *"climb mount kilimanjaro tanzania safari tours"* and 187 times for *"climb mount meru"* — with zero clicks, purely because of position. This is won with authority (links) and by adding the specific sub-topic content people search for (route distance, elevation).
2. **Grow the Google-search share of traffic.** Only 21 of our visitors came from search; the rest is direct and social. This is the muscle SEO is built to grow, and it follows rankings.
3. **Activate reviews — our single biggest ceiling.** Competitors win on review count. With the Business Profile now sorted, every finished trip should trigger a review request. This is the highest-leverage thing we can start immediately.
